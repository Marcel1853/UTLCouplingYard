---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.0.1
Date: 2026-10-21
  Features:
    - New UTL station roles: wagon yard, shunting depot, turnaround.
    - Wagon yard: trains leave all wagons behind their locomotives and pick up the wagons waiting ahead of them, then carry on with their schedule. Mode per station: leave and pick up, only leave, only pick up.
    - Every coupling is announced to UTL, so deliveries and schedules survive it.
    - Shunting depot checks that shunting locomotives drive both ways.
    - Shunting: a wagon yard can name a target wagon yard (right away, when empty or when full). A free shunting locomotive fetches the wagons left there and brings them to the target, using a turnaround if needed.
    - Shunting picks a free target track among wagon yards with the same name and pushes the wagons in (via a turnaround if needed).
    - Relay: signal "Relay" with value N at a requester or provider routes its deliveries via the wagon yard with relay number N. The delivery stays with the wagons; the next train picking them up delivers it.
    - Wagon yard: a train that pushes its wagons in leaves them there and backs off (needs a locomotive facing backwards). Handover on a single track with two stops facing each other.
    - Wagon yard: "Control train limit" – the stop sets its train limit to 0 while wagons block it (leave) or none wait ahead (pick up).
    - Map setting "Wagon yard: move up to waiting wagons" (default off): without it, trains couple only when they stop right at the wagons.
    - Wagons already standing at a wagon yard are registered when the mod starts (remote function "scan").
    - Scenario "UTL Coupling Yard – shunting yard": large shunting yard with real loading and unloading, demo tracks.
  Info:
    - No timers while nothing is being shunted.
